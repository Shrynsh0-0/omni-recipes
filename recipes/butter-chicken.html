Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken (thighs or breasts), cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt or Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon or lime juice
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (or grated ginger and garlic)
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder (for vibrant color and mild heat)
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil or melted butter for cooking the chicken

Directions

  1. Step 1: Marinate the chicken: Mix the yogurt, lemon/lime juice, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, Kashmiri chili powder, turmeric, and salt in a bowl. Coat the chicken pieces evenly and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (or up to several hours).
  2. Step 2: Cook the chicken: Sear the marinated chicken in a skillet with a little oil or butter until browned and mostly cooked through, or broil/bake it until lightly charred. Set aside.
  3. Step 3: Build the tomato base: Melt butter in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the onion (if using) until soft and translucent, then add ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant. Stir in tomato purée/passata (and cashews, if using), salt, and spices. Simmer for 10-15 minutes until thickened.
  4. Step 4: Blend for smoothness (optional): Cool slightly, blend into a smooth sauce, and strain through a fine mesh sieve for a velvety restaurant-style texture.
  5. Step 5: Simmer and finish: Return the sauce to low heat, stir in the cooked chicken, cream, sugar or honey, and crushed kasuri methi. Simmer gently for 5-10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the flavors meld. Garnish with cilantro and serve hot with naan or steamed rice.
