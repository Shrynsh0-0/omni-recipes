Ingredients

  • Potato filling: 350-500 g boiled potatoes (mashed), 6-7 garlic cloves, 1-2 green chilies, 1-inch ginger piece, 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, a pinch of asafoetida (hing), 8-10 curry leaves, 1/8-1/4 tsp turmeric powder, 2 tbsp chopped coriander, salt to taste, and 1 tbsp oil.
  • Batter: 1 cup besan (gram flour), 2 tbsp rice flour (optional, for crispiness), 1/4-1/2 tsp salt, a pinch of hing and turmeric, a pinch of baking soda (optional), and water as needed.
  • Assembly: Soft pav buns, green chutney, dry garlic chutney (sukha masala) or sweet chutney, salted fried green chilies, and oil for deep frying.

Directions

  1. Step 1: Make the chili-garlic paste: Coarsely grind or crush the garlic cloves, green chilies, and ginger.
  2. Step 2: Cook the potato filling: Heat 1 tbsp oil, splutter mustard seeds, then add hing and curry leaves. Stir in the chili-garlic paste and turmeric, followed by the mashed potatoes, coriander, and salt. Mix well, cool completely, and shape into balls.
  3. Step 3: Prepare the batter: Whisk besan, rice flour, salt, hing, and turmeric with water a little at a time until smooth and flowing, yet thick enough to coat. Stir in a pinch of baking soda just before frying if using.
  4. Step 4: Fry the vadas: Heat oil to medium-hot (around 180-190°C). Dip each potato ball in the batter, let excess drip off, and fry in small batches until golden brown. Fry a few whole green chilies in the oil and sprinkle with salt.
  5. Step 5: Assemble and serve: Slit a pav bun without cutting all the way through. Spread green chutney and dry garlic or sweet chutney inside, place a hot vada in the center, and serve immediately with fried chilies.
