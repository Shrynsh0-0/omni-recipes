Ingredients

  • 1½ to 2 cups thick poha & 1-2 finely chopped onions.
  • 2-3 green chilies & ½ cup roasted peanuts.
  • 1 tbsp oil & ¾ tsp mustard seeds.
  • ¾ tsp cumin seeds & ¼ to ½ tsp turmeric.
  • curry leaves, salt & optional sugar.
  • lemon juice & fresh coriander.

Steps

  1. Step 1: Rinse the poha gently in a colander and drain without soaking so the flakes stay fluffy.
  2. Step 2: Heat oil and temper mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and curry leaves until they splutter.
  3. Step 3: Sauté green chilies and chopped onions until soft, then stir in turmeric and salt.
  4. Step 4: Fold in the drained poha and roasted peanuts, cover and steam on low heat for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Step 5: Turn off the heat, squeeze in lemon juice, and garnish with coriander.
